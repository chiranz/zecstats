How big is a transparent Zcash transaction?

Half of the 292,618 transparent transactions between 11 Jul and 10 Oct 2026 moved $1,558 or less. The average is far higher because a few giant transfers carry most of the money.

Half of the 292,618 transactions in this range moved less than $1,558; half moved more. Nine in ten moved $219K or less.

The mean is $259K — 166× the median — because the top 0.46% of transactions (over $10M each) carry half of all the dollars. The median is the number that describes an ordinary transaction.

What this measures, and what it cannot

One data point is one transaction: the total it paid to transparent outputs, priced at that day’s ZEC close. Coinbase (mining reward) transactions are left out — they are issuance, not anyone moving money.

Views. “Transparent → transparent” counts transactions with only transparent inputs. “Leaving a shielded pool” counts deshields. “Everything” combines both.

Change is included, so every figure is an upper bound on what was paid. Spending a 100 ZEC coin to pay someone 1 ZEC sends 99 ZEC back to the sender as change, and the chain does not mark which output is which — so that transaction reads as ~100 ZEC moved.

Rounding. Percentiles are exact over the range. The histogram groups sizes into quarter-decade bands, so readings off the chart are approximate; the tiles above are the precise values.
