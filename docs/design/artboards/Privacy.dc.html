How do round trips give people away?

500 round trips shielded an unusual amount, then deshielded a near-identical one within 7 days — enough to link both transparent ends.

  • ZEC match123
  • USD match197
  • Both180

How to avoid it

  • 1Shield a common round amount from the table, not an odd one like 546.3379.
  • 2Don't deshield the same amount, or the same dollar value, you shielded.
  • 3Let time pass and spend in different-sized pieces.

Heuristic matches (within 0.5%, $1,000 floor) from pool-level flows — strongly suggestive, not proof of ownership.

How these privacy numbers are computed

Pool balances and every shield/deshield flow are exact: they come from consensus-enforced per-block pool totals on our Zebra node (v6.3.0). What stays hidden is everything inside a pool — who holds what, and who pays whom. Transparent-activity buckets, round-trip matches and the suspicion index are heuristics built on top of those exact totals, and are labelled as such. Dollar values use $1,221.62 (11 Oct 2026).
