Iteration 3 · Navigation D, every state

  • Global bar on every page: Dashboard · Privacy · Charts · Tools ▾ · News, a search field (⌘K), the data-status pill, theme and Donate. At 1020px and below the links and field fold into a search icon and the menu.
  • Pill: a live dot and “data through 10 Oct”; a dot only at 640px and below. It opens the status popover: node, block, data through, and this page’s update rhythm.
  • The event tape sits directly under the bar, on every page.
  • Context bar only where a page has siblings: Privacy pages get tabs, chart and tool pages get a group switcher and ‹ ›. Dashboard, News and the Charts index have none.
  • Page header = H1 + one-sentence answer. No kicker, crumbs or freshness column; chart pages add the key figure on the right.

How big is a transparent Zcash transaction?

Half of the 292,618 transparent transactions between 11 Jul and 10 Oct 2026 moved $1,558 or less. The average is far higher because a few giant transfers carry most of the money.

Median · transparent → transparent · 11 Jul – 10 Oct 2026

A typical transparent transaction moves

$1,558median

Half of the 292,618 transactions in this range moved less than $1,558; half moved more. Nine in ten moved $219K or less.

The mean is $259K — 166× the median — because the top 0.46% of transactions (over $10M each) carry half of all the dollars.
