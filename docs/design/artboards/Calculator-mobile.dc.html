What this calculator assumes

Real closing prices. Each purchase uses that day's closing price. It is a replay of what happened, not a smoothed or fitted estimate.

Same schedule for all. Comparison assets are bought on Zcash's purchase dates, so every line has the same number of buys and the same total invested.

Stock indexes are total-return. SPY and QQQ use Yahoo Finance's dividend-reinvested series, not plain price.

Nothing on top. Exchange fees, spreads and taxes are not included. Past returns say nothing about future ones.
