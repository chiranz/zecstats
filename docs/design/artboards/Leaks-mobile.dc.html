How to avoid this — shield and deshield round amounts other people use

An amount like 546.3379 ZEC is a fingerprint; 1, 2.5 or 10 ZEC hides you among thousands of identical transactions. And don’t take the same amount back out a few hours later.

How a leak is detected, and what it cannot prove

A shielded hop only buys privacy if the amount doesn’t fingerprint you. An incident is a transaction that shielded an unusual (non-round) amount and was followed by a deshield of a near-identical amount (within 0.5%) within 7 days. We match on both the ZEC amount and the dollar value, with a floor of $1,000.

This is heuristic inference from net pool value flows, not proof. Amounts are per-transaction shield and deshield legs, and we link to the containing block (the exact txid isn’t stored). A near-exact match on an arbitrary amount is very unlikely to be coincidence, but it can be.
