Five-year outlook · ZEC vs Bitcoin

As of 11 October 2026, one ZEC buys 0.01474 BTC — up 7.1× over five years, up 5.6× on the year and up 76% over three months. Five years ago it bought 0.002071 BTC.

Read more

In dollars that is $1,221.62 a coin and a market cap of $20.78B; fully diluted, ZEC is worth 1.48% of Bitcoin. Spot sits 3.46× above the realized price of $354 — the average price at which a ZEC last crossed a pool boundary — so the typical coin is held at a gain.

Behind it: 29.3% of all ZEC — 4.97M ZEC, $6.08B — sits in shielded pools, against 23.5% a year ago and 7.9% five years ago. Ironwood, the newest pool, holds 4.11M ZEC; 361,823 ZEC is still in Orchard, its predecessor.

16.98M of 21M ZEC has been issued (80.9%). Hashrate is 32.63 GH/s.
