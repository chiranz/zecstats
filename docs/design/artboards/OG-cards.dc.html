Social cards: one template, three variants

  • Size 1200 × 630 (Open Graph and X large card). Content stays 56 px clear of every edge, so rounded crops never cut it.
  • Always dark. The card is the site's identity on someone else's timeline: near-black green ground, the signal-bars mark, the wordmark in neon with its glow.
  • Reads in one glance: section kicker, the page's question, the key figure large in mono with its ▲/▼ change, then the data date and the address.
  • Built from data at build time for every chart (58), the Privacy hub and Ironwood; every other page uses the default card.
  • Type: JetBrains Mono for the wordmark, question and figures; a sans for the one-line answer. Weight 400 throughout (the offline font file is the regular cut).
