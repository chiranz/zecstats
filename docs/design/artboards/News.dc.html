What's a σ?

σ (sigma) says how unusual a value is next to its own last 30 days. +2.8σ means today sits 2.8 times the usual spread above the 30-day average.

If moves were normally distributed, beyond ±2σ would happen about 1 day in 20, and beyond ±3σ about 1 day in 370. Crypto often isn't normal, so read σ as a ranking, not a probability.
