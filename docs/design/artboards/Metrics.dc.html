How these numbers are computed

Levels and rates. Levels such as supply and price are end-of-day values. Rates such as transactions per day are 7-day averages, so one busy day doesn't swing them.

One source of truth. Every row comes from the same daily rollup of our Zebra full node that powers the charts, so this table and the chart pages always agree.

Price is the one external input: Coin Metrics, then CoinGecko, Coinbase and Kraken as fallbacks. Derivatives come from Hyperliquid; cross-chain flows from NEAR Intents.

Complete days only. Today's partial day is left out until it closes (UTC). Your columns and metric choices are kept in the link and in this browser.
