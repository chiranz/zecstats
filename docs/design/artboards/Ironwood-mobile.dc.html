How much supply is verified?

97.9% of all 16,979,486 ZEC now sits outside Orchard, where the 2026 soundness bug cannot have hidden counterfeit coins.

Every pool except the un-migrated Orchard balance counts as verified supply: coins leaving Orchard must cross the turnstile, where consensus checks the pool never pays out more than it holds. The remaining 361,823 ZEC still in Orchard is what has yet to cross. See the Turnstile watch.

What that percentage counts, and what it leaves out

Crossings, not holdingsOrchard onlyA fixed denominator ShowHide

Ironwood is the newest official Zcash shielded pool, live since block 3,428,143. This page tracks the turnstile migration: value leaving the older Orchard pool and re-shielding into Ironwood. The headline is Orchard coins that have crossed into Ironwood, divided by Orchard's balance at the activation block.

  • Crossings, not holdingsCoins that crossed and later left Ironwood still count.
  • Orchard onlyTransparent and Sapling coins have flowed into Ironwood too. They show up in "Migrated from all pools", the Ironwood balance and the source breakdown below.
  • A fixed denominatorOrchard's balance at the activation block, never today's. An ordinary Orchard→transparent spend moves neither half of the fraction, so the percentage cannot climb on days when nothing migrated.

Every transaction that has moved value into Ironwood since activation, from all source pools — one point per transaction. Read it as: y% of migrations were this size or smaller. The axis is logarithmic because sizes span nine orders of magnitude.

The average migration is 47.5 ZEC, more than 300× the median. The smallest 25% are under 0.0198 ZEC, while the largest 1% are over 760.3 ZEC. So a median migration is not a typical migrated coin: most coins crossed in a few very large transactions.

How these numbers are computed

Pool balances come from consensus-enforced per-block pool totals on our Zebra node (v6.3.0), so they are exact. Source attribution is per transaction: the value entering Ironwood is assigned to the pool(s) that funded the same transaction. Dollar figures use the 11 Oct 2026 price of $1,221.62 for every row, so they show what the ZEC is worth now, not what it was worth when it moved.
