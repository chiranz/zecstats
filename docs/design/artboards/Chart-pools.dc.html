How do you read the shielded pools?

Zcash has had four shielded pools, and they are generations, not alternatives. Sprout (2016) was the original and is deprecated; Sapling (2018) made shielded transactions practical; Orchard (NU5, 2022) replaced it; Ironwood is the newest. Value moves forward through them, so a falling Sapling line is usually migration, not people giving up on privacy.

Every crossing is visible even though the contents are not. A shielded pool’s total is enforced by consensus: value entering or leaving crosses the transparent turnstile and is counted, so these balances are exact rather than estimated. What stays hidden is everything inside — who holds it, in what amounts, and who it moves to.

The handover at the right edge is Ironwood. Since it went live on 28 Jul 2026 (block 3,428,143), 89.24% of the 3,661,233 ZEC Orchard held at activation has crossed into it.

What this is not: a measure of how much Zcash activity is private. A balance is a stock; for activity, see transaction types and the shielded share of transactions.

Methodology & caveats
  • Tooltip totals add only the pools that are switched on; hidden series never count.
  • A pool appears in the tooltip only after it launched — no “Ironwood: 0” rows before Jul 2026.
  • Small multiples give each pool its own y-axis, so Sprout’s 22,309 ZEC is readable.
  • The CSV has one column per pool, including Transparent, whatever is switched on.
