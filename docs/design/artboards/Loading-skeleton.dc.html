Loading: the answer skeleton

  • While a page is on its way, the page area shows that page’s own layout with every line of text as a shimmering bar. It appears at once: it has the page’s shape, so nothing jumps when the page lands.
  • Built from the pages’ own layout classes, so it changes at the same widths as the page: 560, 640, 900, 980, 1000 and 1020 px (KPI bands 4 → 2, key figure hidden on phones, rails folded).
  • Six shapes, chosen by the address being opened: dashboard, chart page, report, news, charts index, and one for every other page.
  • Below: the real page on the left and its skeleton on the right, same window, same top bar, at laptop, tablet and phone widths.

Text
Each line keeps its real font and wrap and is painted as a bar about the height of its letters. Filler of the real length sits inside, invisible.
Blocks
Plots, sparklines, icons and swatches are solid blocks at their real size; plots follow the chart’s own rule (70% of the card’s width on phones, at least 220 px).
Shimmer
One sweep for the whole page, 1.6 s, left to right; every bar catches it at the same moment.
Signal mark
The site’s logo beside the title; its three bars rise in turn, 150 ms apart, on a 1.2 s loop.
Progress
A 2 px green line on the top edge of the window grows from the left, fast then slower, and stops short of the end; the page replaces it when it lands. (The demo here repeats; on the site it runs once.)
Kept
Top bar, event tape and context bar stay live; only the page area changes.
Reduced motion
No shimmer, the line holds still, bars at full signal.
Screen readers
One polite “Loading” status; the bars are hidden from assistive tech.
