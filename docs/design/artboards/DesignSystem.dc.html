Principles

The answer comes first

Every page opens with the number it exists to show. Methodology and caveats fold away at the bottom — never above the chart.

Neon is a signal, not a texture

#00ff66 marks the brand, the active state, the primary series and "up". Body copy is soft mint-white; glow lives only on the wordmark.

One colour, one meaning

Orchard is violet on every chart on every page. Transparent is neutral grey ("not private"). ZEC is green, Bitcoin is orange.

Full rows, no orphans

KPI bands hold 4, 6 or 8 tiles. Content spans the 1200px shell — no narrow left column beside empty space.

One control per job

A single range control (1M 3M 6M 1Y 5Y ALL), one segmented style, one chip style, one tooltip — on every page.

Mono for data, sans for reading

JetBrains Mono keeps the terminal voice in headings, labels and figures. IBM Plex Sans carries paragraphs at 14–17px.

Do
✓Columns for counts and period totals (tx/day, fees per week) with a rolling-average line.
✓Log toggle for price and ratios over 1Y+; label the scale as log.
✓Fixed 0–100% axis for shares (shielding rate).
✓Direct end-labels for 4 or fewer series; legend-as-filter beyond that.
✓Clip to the plot; per-chart default range (supply opens on ALL, price on 1Y).
✓Stacked panels sharing one x-axis when units differ.
Don't
✕Smoothed splines on daily data — they invent peaks.
✕Rainbow on ordered buckets (age, depth, hops).
✕More than 5 hues — highlight ZEC, grey the rest, or use small multiples.
✕Red vs green only — use position (above/below zero) or blue/orange.
✕Two "latest" boxes or a headline dated differently from the plot.
✕"20000k", "1e-6", rotated ticks — write 20M, per $1M, Oct '26.
